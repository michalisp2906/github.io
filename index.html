UK Fuel Pricing Fairness

https://github.com/michalisp2906/fuel-fairness-project https://fuel-fairness-project.streamlit.app/

A peer-relative model that flags fuel stations charging more than local market conditions justify. Built on a DuckDB medallion pipeline with GeoPandas spatial features, a LightGBM model validated with spatial cross-validation, and SHAP for explainability. Deployed live with Streamlit.

LightGBMDuckDB GeoPandasSHAP Streamlit

NYC Property Price Prediction

https://github.com/michalisp2906/ny-real-estate-prediction

An ARIMA and XGBoost ensemble trained on 8,200 New York listings, reaching an R-squared of 0.742. Combines KNN imputation for missing values with NLP features derived from listing text using sentence embeddings, tuned with GridSearchCV.

XGBoostARIMA NLPGridSearchCV

DVLA Vehicle Registration Trends

https://github.com/michalisp2906/car_market_model

A time-series study of UK vehicle registration data, using ARIMA forecasting to explore how vehicle types and fuel categories have shifted over time.
